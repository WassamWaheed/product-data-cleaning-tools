# Product Data Quality Checklist

- [ ] SKUs are unique and normalized
- [ ] Titles have no accidental leading/trailing spaces
- [ ] Brand values are consistent
- [ ] Required identifiers are present
- [ ] Prices are numeric and valid
- [ ] Empty values are standardized
- [ ] Variation fields use consistent naming
- [ ] Category-specific attributes are reviewed
- [ ] Duplicate product records are flagged
- [ ] Changes are logged before marketplace upload
